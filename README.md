1. Plan Your Project 
Project Name 
Suprise Essence 
Target Audience 
The target audience for Surprise Essence is teenagers, young adults and adults who enjoy 
fragrances and want affordable, quality scents for everyday use and special occasions. The 
website is aimed at both men and women who are interested in discovering and purchasing 
fragrance essences online. 
Project Goals 
Main goals of the Surprise Essence website are to: 
• Introduce customers to the Surprise Essence brand. 
• Display available fragrance products and their prices. 
• Provide customers with useful information about each product. 
• Make it easy for customers to navigate through the website. 
• Provide contact information for customers who want to make enquiries or place orders. 
• Create a professional online presence for Surprise Essence. 
Website Content 
The website will contain the following sections: 
• Home – Welcomes visitors to Surprise Essence. 
• About Us – Provides information about the business. 
• Products – Displays the available fragrance essences and prices. 
• Gallery – Displays images of the products. 
• Contact Us – Provides contact information. 
• Footer – Contains copyright information.
